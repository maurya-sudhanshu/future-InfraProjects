WATER & ENVIRONMENT

Deep in innovation

With the phenomenal and incessant growth in population, urbanisation and rapid industrialisation, the need for water has overtaken its supply.

We have made our presence felt through significant water and sanitation projects - including the unique Limca Book of Records entry, the 820 kms.’ gravity-based water supply system in Amaravati, Maharashtra. Over the next few years, water supply and sanitation projects are expected to witness significant outlays. Our experience and excellent credentials will help us cruise through this space successfully.

Management of water and waste water has assumed enormous significance and NCC plays a crucial role in this field. NCC has augmented its expertise to be able to execute various water supply systems, drainage systems and sewage treatment plants through specialised systems for high value projects.
