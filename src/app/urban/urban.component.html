Expanding through subsidiaries

In order to capitalise on the synergies of skills and concentrate on specific areas of operation, NCC Urban is one of the subsidiaries floated based on the activities undertaken.

NCC URBAN

Redefining the urban landscape

NCC Urban Infrastructure Ltd. is a subsidiary of NCC Limited. Its quality operations span across residential and commercial complexes, townships, SEZs and serviced apartment complexes. The company has an impressive pan India presence across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi, Guntur and Ranchi. The Company has also acquired lands in and around Bengaluru, Chennai, Goa, Gurgaon, Hyderabad, Kakinada, Lucknow, Raipur and Vizag for its real estate projects. NCC Urban is all set to deliver 15.8 million sft. of built-up area spread over 286 acres in the next three years.
